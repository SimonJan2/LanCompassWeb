The topology arc 2, "A map that tells the truth", and its
expansion, the design studio: everything since 0.6.1
(`e01081d7`). Six database migrations (`0113_connected_seen_at` →
`0118_design_palettes`) run on the first start. No new dependency. On
the arc's lab network the map's precision went from 0.386 to 1.0 (17 of
17 links drawn, none false), and the studio's twelve journeys were walked
in Hebrew, then English.

### The map

- **One line per cable, and a host on its real port.** LLDP
  and CDP, both ends' reports and a CDP long port name
  ("GigabitEthernet0/24" for "Gi0/24") merge into one line per cable. A
  name two devices share is refused, naming the candidates, instead of
  guessed. A neighbour listed on
  a port that is down confirms nothing ("Listed by the switch, but the port
  is down"). A host is placed only on an edge port: no cable to a switch,
  no forwarding neighbour, not an aggregate, at most 8 MACs (Settings →
  Features → SNMP enrichment, 1–64). A device an uplink sees is drawn on
  the **Behind a port (inferred)** layer, dotted grey and off by default;
  it never counts as a cable, a ghost or a path hop.
- **Every line says how old it is.** A line's age is its own newest
  sighting; a documented cable says "Verified on the wire … ago" or "Never
  verified on the wire". A line with no sighting for 7 days draws grey,
  and an observed line leaves the canvas after 30 (its card and Review keep
  it; documented cables only grey) — Settings → Features → Topology. The
  World shows VPN tunnel state and "Record as of …" on every WAN line;
  exports and the A3 print carry the evidence line in their stamp.
- **Gone and changed, on the map.** A bundle is verified only when
  every cable in it is ("1 of 2 cables verified"). The Changes lens shows
  a cable not seen since a date, a moved host and a moved VM. A cable that
  leaves the wire reaches Discovery → Gone ("Cable no longer seen on the
  wire"). Proposed cables draw amber and dashed: select several on the map,
  preview, apply as one batch, undo in one step.
- **Ask the map.** Press Q on a canvas and ask "where is …" (an
  address, MAC or name → its switch port and age), "path from … to …"
  (across sites at layer 3, through the gateways and the VPN) or "what
  breaks if …" (the devices cut off, those kept and the links keeping
  them). **Show on map** opens a device — from its page and from its
  cables, rack, subnet, VLAN, circuit, monitor, incident and Review rows.
- **The connections still missing.** LAG membership read from the
  switch draws a LAG as one line ("LAG · 2 × 10G"); a port a person
  documented keeps its value and is flagged when the wire disagrees. A VM
  without a site is drawn beside its host. Every circuit is on the World,
  which says how many WAN links it left out when it is capped.
- **A layout that stays put.** Re-laying a canvas moves no node it
  already had ("Re-laid — 0 nodes moved"); saved positions never move.
  Device cards with names on two lines, a World of 40 or more sites in a
  grid by WAN hub, and fits that keep clear of the rail, legend and
  minimap. A first visit lands on the busiest site. Only a canvas's owner
  (or an admin, on a built-in canvas) saves its layout; **Save as my
  canvas** keeps your own.
- **The map in an outage.** A device whose monitor is down draws
  down within one sweep ("Monitor: down since …"); its site rolls up
  offline on the World, and its tunnels draw down. A site incident names
  its root: "Root: {device} — {n} more unreachable behind it". Settings →
  Features → Topology can keep the devices behind a down root from sending
  their own notifications, per site (off by default; still recorded).
- **The last two truths.** A desk phone or station that reports
  itself as one over LLDP (and not as a bridge) is a host on its port,
  never a ghost line. A host the switch sees on another port than the
  documented one opens a proposal in Discovery ("1 link the switch
  contradicts" → "Move the link to Gi0/7"), applied and undone in one step.

### The design studio

- **Quicker and faithful.** A 500-object paste takes about 0.6–0.7 s
  (was about 1.2 s) and its undo about 0.3 s (was up to 0.7 s); a drag
  stays at 60 frames a second. A `.drawio` import is saved when you close
  it, as one version ("Imported from {file}"); a Visio or draw.io import
  closed with nothing imported leaves no empty design. **Show as table**
  lists both canvases' nodes and links for the keyboard and screen readers.
- **Connection points on demand.** An object shows its connection
  points when you hover it, select it alone, long-press it or connect near
  it; at rest none are drawn, so a 2,000-object design opens in about 3.3 s
  (was 3.8 s). To move a link's end, click the link first. A connection
  point under another object still takes the press, through a small window
  in the object on top; nothing changes which object paints on top.
- **Every object its own look.** The inspector's **Style** section:
  fill, outline and its width, transparency, shadow, italic, underline and
  formatted labels (`**bold**`, `*italic*`), and each end of a link its own
  head (arrow, open, diamond, circle, bar or none); **Reset the style to the
  theme** undoes it. Own colours hold in every theme but the two high
  contrast ones, forced colours and the new **Print in theme colours**.
  Visio and draw.io imports keep a file's own colours, line weights,
  shadows, text styles and line ends.
- **Hand it back as Visio.** **Export → Visio (.vsdx)**: a page per
  frame, connectors glued at both ends, the styles, Hebrew and Arabic right
  to left, each object's fields as Shape Data, layers and groups. A file
  LanCompass wrote comes back into the studio as it left, every position
  within a pixel.
- **Comments and who is here.** **Add comment** on an object, a link
  or a point; reply, resolve, reopen. Anyone who can open the design can
  comment, a viewer too; only the author edits a comment, and the author,
  the design's owner or an admin resolves or deletes it. A comment whose
  object is deleted stays, marked **Object removed**. Pins never print or
  export. The toolbar names everyone else in the design.
- **Edit together.** People who can edit a design edit it at the
  same time: each sees the others' changes within about a quarter of a
  second, their selections and their pointers. Undo takes back only your
  own steps. Save keeps everyone's changes ("Saved by {name}"); **Discard
  everyone's unsaved changes** asks first and names whose go. A lost
  connection pauses live editing and catches up when it is back.
- **Designs that stay true.** A device copied from the map stays
  bound to it: on opening, "{n} changes in the inventory since you last
  looked" — Preview, Apply (one undoable step) or Not now. A label you
  typed is yours and is never refreshed; a device the inventory lost is
  marked **Gone from the inventory**, never removed. **Import → Refresh
  from the map** brings in the devices and links the map gained or lost,
  in one step. **Sign** a version in Versions:
  it cannot be deleted while signed, the reality lens shows what changed
  **Since signed**, and its print carries the signature.
- **Racks on the canvas.** Draw an inventory rack with its devices
  (read-only; **Open rack** opens its elevation) or plan one (1–60 U) and
  drop devices onto their units — a taken unit is refused ("U40 is taken by
  …"). Promote creates the planned rack and its devices; `.drawio` and
  Visio carry a rack as a group.
- **Pen and pointer.** **Pen** (P), **Highlighter** (M) and
  **Eraser** (E) draw over the design; a stylus draws while a finger pans.
  In presentation mode the **Laser pointer** draws a fading trail on
  everyone's screen with the presenter's name; it is never saved.
- **Say it, link it.** Start a design **From text (Mermaid)** or
  **From CSV files** (a devices file and a links file): every line left
  out is listed by number, nothing is guessed. A shape links to another
  design, a frame or a web address (badge or Ctrl/⌘-click). **Pages** in
  the layers drawer orders the frames for presenting and the Visio export.
- **More shapes, and yours.** **Cloud & services**: 24 generic
  types (regions, virtual networks and subnets as containers, load
  balancers, storage, databases, queues, functions, clusters, key vaults
  and more), found by name or by product words ("lambda", "s3"), never
  promoted into the inventory. **Flowchart** (12 shapes) and 8 more basic
  shapes, which go out to draw.io and Visio as those tools' own shapes.
  **Import a stencil (.vssx or a diagrams.net library)**: a team's stencil
  becomes a palette under **My stencils**, shared under **Shared stencils**
  unless you untick **Shared with everyone**. The import lists every shape
  it left out and why, and refuses a file of more than 2,000 parts or 50 MB
  unpacked before it unpacks anything. Deleting a stencil never takes a
  shape away from a design.

### Fixed

- **A link dropped on an object lands on that object at any zoom.** Below
  or above 100 %, or after a pan, a link let go on an object's body went to
  another object, opened the quick-create box or drew nothing.
- **A dense design saves.** A design of several hundred objects could be
  refused on Save because of its thumbnail; the thumbnail is now made to
  fit, and an empty picture is used when it cannot.
- **A fresh browser lands on a site, not the World.** The map's first
  visit fell through to the World and remembered it.

### Upgrading from 0.6.1

- **As for 0.6.0:** extract the bundle into `incoming/`, run its
  `upgrade.sh` from your install directory (`INSTALL.md` §6) and compare
  the fingerprint it prints with `INSTALL.md` §1. The backup it takes
  first is your rollback file; a rollback ends by restoring it, so work
  done on 0.7.0 does not go back to 0.6.1.
- **Six migrations, all additions:** a sighting time on addresses, LAG
  membership on interfaces, an incident's root, design comments, signed
  versions and stencil palettes. A 0.6.1 backup restores on 0.7.0, and
  0.7.0's backups include the new tables.
- **Ages start at the first poll after the upgrade.** Until a poll has seen
  a line, the map cannot say how old it is, and a documented cable reads
  "Never verified on the wire".
- **Hosts move to their access ports.** After the first poll, a host 0.6.1
  drew on an uplink is drawn on its edge port, or only on the **Behind a
  port (inferred)** layer, which is off by default.
- **Behaviour that changes for existing designs:** own colours and hex
  accents give way in the high-contrast themes and in **Print in theme
  colours**; presentation follows the frames' reading order until you
  reorder **Pages**; a link's end moves only after the link is clicked;
  copies from the map made before 0.7.0 keep their labels as they are.
- **Live editing and presence run through the web tier** as a server-sent
  event stream, with unsaved shared changes kept in Redis for 24 hours. A
  proxy of your own in front of LanCompass must pass `text/event-stream`
  unbuffered (the stream sends `X-Accel-Buffering: no` and `Cache-Control:
  no-transform`); without the stream, Save falls back to an ordinary save
  after 30 seconds.
- **Stencils count as design icons.** Their shapes count toward **Max icon
  count** (Settings → Features → Design Studio, default 500, up to 5,000):
  raise it before importing a large stencil.

### Known issues

What a pilot can still meet on this release:

- **"Discard everyone's unsaved changes" sits over the top centre of the
  canvas** while a design has unsaved changes, and takes presses meant for
  what lies under it. Workaround: Save, which removes it, or pan the
  canvas down.
- **The "changes in the inventory" notice can cover a design's top row**
  until you choose Not now, Preview or Apply.
- **Cisco IP phones still draw as ghost lines** to their switch: they
  report themselves as a bridge as well as a telephone, and a bridge stays
  a ghost.
- **A Visio file exported, then imported again through the UI, gains a
  legend and re-fitted devices** unless **Add legend** is off in the
  import's review; a design wider than 6,000 px comes back scaled.
- **A wide tree from text lays out as one tall column:** a text whose
  last level holds hundreds of nodes is too tall to fit on screen.
- **After you open or reload a design, the others' selections show only
  when they next change**; a name tag can hide under your own selection
  toolbar.
- **A map copy's device boxes cut long names in print** ("hq-acc-2-…").
- **A full 500-shape stencil fills a default install's icons** — see
  Upgrading.
- **The Scans page's live progress can arrive only when a scan ends:** the
  web tier's compression holds that stream (the scan troubleshooter's is
  not affected). Older than this release.
- **Smaller:** a link move's preview names interface ids
  ("connected_interface_id : 7 → 52") under its sentence; a link let go
  right next to a selected link's free end draws nothing; a phone the
  inventory lacks can still be offered as a cable to make; the demo
  World's site cards cut their counts line at 100 %; the Hebrew docs say
  "יציאה" where the screens say "פורט"; in Hebrew, a user's language change
  shows the raw field name "language" on `/changelog`.
- **0.6.0's known issues outside the map and the design studio still
  apply,** F1 among them (the docs, NetBox and workbook imports inflate
  each zip member whole). The new stencil import is read in the browser
  and capped.

## The standard we follow

This site is built to meet Israeli Standard 5568 Part 1 (2023), at its AA level (WCAG 2.0 with national changes), the standard the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities regulations refer to; it is also tested against WCAG 2.2 AA. Every page was last checked on <time datetime="2026-11-07">7 November 2026</time>.

## The adjustments we made

- Every page exists in English and in Hebrew, with the correct language and reading direction.
- The site works with a keyboard alone. A “Skip to content” link is the first thing on every page, every interactive element shows a visible focus ring, and the header that stays at the top of the screen never covers the element in focus.
- Links and buttons outside running text are at least 24 × 24 pixels, or have that much room around them.
- Help is always in the same place: the footer of every page links to the contact page.
- Pages use headings, landmarks and lists so screen readers can move through them.
- Images have text alternatives in both languages; decorative images are hidden from assistive technology.
- Text contrast is at least 4.5:1, and colour is never the only way information is shown.
- Animation is calm and can be paused. If your system asks for reduced motion, the site shows still images instead.
- The product videos have no sound. Each has captions in English and in Hebrew, and [the videos page](/videos/) lists, as text, the steps each one shows.
- Forms have visible labels, say which fields are required, and explain errors in text. They ask for each thing only once and never ask you to solve a puzzle.
- The site works without JavaScript.

The latest accessibility review of this site is the automated check of 7 November 2026: every page, in both languages and in each of the site’s three themes (light, dark, and the one that follows your device), was tested against WCAG 2.2 AA and walked with the keyboard, and the main pages were checked at phone width too.

## Known limitations

- Product screenshots show the LanCompass interface. The text alternative describes each screen, not every value in it.

## Contact us about accessibility

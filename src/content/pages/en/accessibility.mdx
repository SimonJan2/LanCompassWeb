## The standard we follow

This site is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. The Israeli Standard IS 5568, which the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities regulations refer to, is based on the same guidelines.

## What we did

- Every page exists in English and in Hebrew, with the correct language and reading direction.
- The site works with a keyboard alone. A “Skip to content” link is the first thing on every page, and every interactive element shows a visible focus ring.
- Pages use headings, landmarks and lists so screen readers can move through them.
- Images have text alternatives in both languages; decorative images are hidden from assistive technology.
- Text contrast is at least 4.5:1, and colour is never the only way information is shown.
- Animation is calm and can be paused. If your system asks for reduced motion, the site shows still images instead.
- Forms have visible labels, say which fields are required, and explain errors in text.
- The site works without JavaScript.

We test every page in both languages with automated accessibility checks at phone and desktop widths, and by keyboard.

## Known limitations

- Product screenshots show the LanCompass interface. The text alternative describes each screen, not every value in it.
- The product videos have captions in English and Hebrew.

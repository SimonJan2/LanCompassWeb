## What this site does not do

- It sets **no cookies** and keeps nothing in your browser to track you.
- It loads **nothing from other sites**: fonts, images and scripts are served from this site’s own address. There are no embeds, trackers or analytics.
- It does not build a profile of you.

Because there are no cookies, there is no cookie banner.

## How to reach us

We take messages by email only; this site has no form. The buttons on [the pilot page](/pilot/#apply) and [the contact page](/contact/) open your mail app with a short template to fill in. For a pilot request it asks for your name, organisation, role, country, the size of your network, your main vendors, whether the network is air-gapped, and a message; for anything else, your name, organisation and a message. Your email address comes with the message. Fill in only what you want to share: what you put in the email is what we receive.

We use it only to answer you and, for a pilot request, to judge whether LanCompass fits your network. We don’t sell it, and we don’t use it for advertising.

## Who delivers this site

Cloudflare delivers this site to you. To deliver and protect it, Cloudflare processes your IP address and the details of your requests, under [its own privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Our servers’ access logs

Our own servers keep access logs, which may include IP addresses. We keep them for at most 30 days.

## How long we keep your messages

We keep your messages for 12 months after the last message, and then delete them.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what you sent us. Write to <ContactEmail subject="Privacy request" />.

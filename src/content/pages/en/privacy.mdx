## What this site does not do

- It sets **no cookies** and uses no local storage to track you.
- It loads **nothing from third parties**: fonts, images and scripts are served from this site. There are no embeds, trackers or analytics.
- It does not build a profile of you.

Because there are no cookies, there is no cookie banner.

## What the forms collect, and why

The design-partner application asks for your name, organisation, role, work email, country, the size of your network, your main vendors, whether the network is air-gapped, and a message. The contact form asks for your name, work email, organisation and a message.

We use this information only to answer you and, for design-partner applications, to judge whether LanCompass fits your network. We don’t sell it, and we don’t use it for advertising.

If you write to us by email instead, we receive what you put in the email.

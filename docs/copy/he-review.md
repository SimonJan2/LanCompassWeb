# Hebrew copy review

For the native Hebrew reviewer. Every Hebrew string on the site, with a literal English back-gloss (what the Hebrew says, not the English page) and a one-word note:

- **kept**: identical to W1.
- **new**: a string W1 did not have (new layout).
- **rewritten: voice** — re-authored in the register of [`he-voice.md`](he-voice.md); **glossary** — a term aligned with the product's own Hebrew interface (קידומת, פאנל פאץ׳, גילוי סביל, תור הסקירה); **address** — form of address fixed (verbal nouns on buttons, first-person neutral consent, no slash forms); **calque** — a translated turn of phrase removed; **length** — headline cut to 3–8 words.

Strings are grouped by file. `copy.*` keys live in `src/content/copy/he.ts`, `ui.*` keys in `src/i18n/he.json`, and the long-form pages in `src/content/pages/he/*.mdx`. Latin-only strings (product names, the integration list) are listed once at the end. Markup (`<bdi>`, `<code>`) is removed here for reading; it stays in the source.

What to look for: anything that sounds translated, any term an Israeli network engineer would not use, and any sentence a reader would stumble on. Mark the row and suggest the Hebrew; the facts must stay the same (each product sentence cites its row of [`docs/claims.md`](../claims.md), read at the product's release tag).

**W2 (2026-11-06)** brought the copy to product 0.7.0: rows marked **new (W2)** are strings W2 added, and **rewritten (W2): facts** are strings whose facts changed at 0.7.0 (the language tiers, the signed bundle, the map, the design studio, the one queue). Terms come from the product's own Hebrew interface at `v0.7.0` where it has one: המפה, לשאול את המפה, אומת ברשת לפני…, מאחורי פורט, סטודיו התכנון, חיפוש ושמירה, תוכנית IP, מציאת מקום, מחזור חיים, מצב קיר, חבילת ראיות, מוחזק — ממתין לאדם. Two choices for the reviewer: the product calls dashboards „דשבורדים” while the site keeps its own „לוחות בקרה”, and it calls the Design Studio both „סטודיו התכנון” and „סטודיו עיצוב” (the site keeps „סטודיו התכנון”).
